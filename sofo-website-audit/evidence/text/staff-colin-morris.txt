About

Updated February 4, 2023

 Colin Morris
 Front of House Duty Manager

My name is Colin, and I am a professional photographer with over 50 years’ of experience. I have previously owned a photographic retail business for over 30 years, around the Oxfordshire area. My work has covered advertising both in digital and printed form. Also, product and archiving for various businesses, museums and organisations across Oxfordshire. under the mentorship of Lawrence Waters, I attained LRPS from the Royal Photographic Society. I also produce product shots that sell. I have always been a passionate photographer outside of my work and I enjoyed travelling and documenting various countries in the world. These include Iceland, Kenya, Italy and the USA, with my specialities being landscape, Natural History motorsport and Reportage Photography. I have a keen interest in local history mainly homefront and photographing 1940s events as a 1940s photographer using film of course.
