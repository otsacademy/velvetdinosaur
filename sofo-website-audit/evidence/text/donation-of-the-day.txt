DONATION OF THE DAY

Today we were donated a leaflet about the multi-use ‘Kredemnon’, a WW2 version of the more modern snood. It seems like it was a very versatile garment – you can even ‘Make a skirt of it while your clothes are drying’!

The leaflet wishes ‘Good Luck’ to those brave soldiers who wanted to try it out…

Do you have any 1930’s – 1970’s items that are related to Oxfordshire soldiers? SOFO are currently collecting for our new exhibition programme in 2019. Contact the museum for more details.
