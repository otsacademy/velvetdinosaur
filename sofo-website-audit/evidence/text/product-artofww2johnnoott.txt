The Art of World War II - A Personal Collection quantity
Add to Cart

A history in pictures of the Second world War, this book features full colour images of all the paintings displayed during  The Art of World War II - A Personal Collection exhibition with commentary on both the art and the history depicted within it, as well as personal notes from collector John Noott.

The exhibition's run at Soldiers of Oxfordshire Museum in 2020 was sadly cut short by the Covid-19 pandemic and resulting national lockdown. Not just a companion, the book is perfect for those that missed out on the exhibition.

Collected and selected by John Noott

Compiled and curated by Steve Marshall

Paperback, 80 Pages
