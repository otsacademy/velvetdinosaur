Become a Sponsor

Help us celebrate our tenth anniversary in 2024 and join us as a sponsor now as we plan our future activities.

As one of our museum sponsors, your company is identified with and provides meaningful support for SOFO’s most visible and significant activities.

We offer a range of exhibition, program, and event sponsorship opportunities that enable you to reach targeted segments of the museum’s audience, as well as your own constituency.

Why Sponsor?

Sponsorship is good for business… your business! Your sponsorship of an exhibition, program or event at SOFO enables you to do the following:

 Tie your product and mission to one of the county’s cultural attractions
 Communicate a message of your community involvement
 Position your organisation as a devoted supporter of the county’s military heritage
 Increase your visibility
 Receive recognition as a sponsor in related print material
 Have greater control over placement of your message than with mass media alone
 Bolster your public relations activities
 Provide corporate entertainment for your clients and employees at the museum with a host of add on activities
 Receive invitations to VIP receptions
 Have access to a variety of audiences within a single venue in the county
 Create a lasting impact
 Provide a meaningful experience for your intended audience
 Show your own supporters and contacts that you are committed to the quality of life of our community by helping to bring people and heritage together

Sponsorship Benefits

We will work with you to specially tailor your benefits to meet the needs and nature of your business. Some of our sponsors have used their association with us to entertain important clients, to offer staff benefits, or to have product opportunities at sponsored events or exhibitions.

The following list is just a sample of available benefits to our museum sponsors depending on the level of sponsorship:

 Recognition in Museum’s newsletter
 Recognition in exhibition-related press releases
 Recognition in accompanying exhibition brochure
 Recognition on Museum’s Web site
 Recognition in Museum’s impact report
 Recognition in Museum reception during sponsorship year
 Company’s name featured in exhibition gallery
 Number of free tickets to exhibition
 One-year subscription to Museum newsletter
 One free rental of Museum’s meeting room during opening hours or special rates during out of hours
 Discounted exclusive use of the museum for events and receptions
 Gift memberships for your employees or clients
 Logo featured on related publications and paid advertisements
 Logo featured on exhibition banner
 One month dedicated to your company which includes signage and free admission to galleries for your employees
 Product opportunity at exhibition opening
 Early opportunity for future sponsorships

Packages

Our sponsorship opportunities range from £550 upwards.

Our annual schools programme can be sponsored from £1500, while temporary exhibitions can be sponsored from £2000.

Contact us at fundraising@sofo.org.uk to find out more and discuss sponsorship opportunities for your business at Soldiers of Oxfordshire Museum
