Oxford Open Doors: Christ Church Cathedral, Chapel of Remembrance Tour | 1030 - 12 September 2026 quantity
Add to Cart

Soldiers of Oxfordshire Museum will be running a tour of the Chapel of Remembrance, the regimental chapel for the county regiments, The Oxfordshire & Buckinghamshire Light Infantry and Oxfordshire Yeomanry.

The tour will last for around 30 minutes, starting at Cathedral porch. The tour will also cover the College War Memorials and other military memorials at the Cathedral and Cloister within the context of county, regimental history and remembrance.

Please meet for the tour at Tom Gate.

This tour is free, but please register your place as spaces are strictly limited.
