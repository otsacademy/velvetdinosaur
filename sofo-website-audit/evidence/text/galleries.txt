Art Submissions

We accept art exhibition submissions on an ongoing basis.

The museum looks at artistic vision and merit, relevance to our audiences, diversity, and budget when considering any proposal.

Individual artists and curators working in any media or discipline are welcome to apply.

Exhibitions may be selected from submitted proposals as well from those initiated by the museum team.

Please contact Ursula Corcoran at the museum for an initial discussion, or email your proposal to:

museumdirector@sofo.org.uk
