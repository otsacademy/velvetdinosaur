Logistics in Afghanistan
Online Talk and Live Q&A with Major General (Retd) David Shouesmith
Available Now (First streamed live :Tuesday 20 February 2024)

Sponsored by Beard Construction

 

LOGISTICS IN AFGHANISTAN

Now Chairman of the Soldiers of Oxfordshire Trust, David Shouesmith's 28-year military career included service as ACDS (Logistic Operations) in the UK MoD and logistic command on operations from squadron to brigade level, from Bosnia to Afghanistan. In this online talk and live Q&A session, David will explore the critical role of logistics and the Royal Logistic Corps throughout his time in Afghanistan, and the unique challenges they faced.

No booking or pre-registration required to view this talk, the video feed will be available on this page once the talk goes live, and will remain available on-demand shortly fater the live event ends.
