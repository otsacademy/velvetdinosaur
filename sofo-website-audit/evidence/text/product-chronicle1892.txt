A chronicle of events and news of the 43rd and & 52nd Light Infantry regiments from 1892, inlcuding maps, illustrations and historical articles. The 43rd & 52nd were predecessors to the Oxfordshire Light Infantry and later Oxfordshire & Buckinghamshire Light Infantry.

Compiled and Edited by Captain A. F. Mockler-Ferryman

Published 1893 - Eyre and Spottiswoode, London

Hardback, 297 pages
