Redcoats to Riflemen: A Short History of the Oxfordshire and Buckinghamshire County Regiment

Description
A history of the Oxfordshire and Buckinghamshire Light Infantry from 1741 all the way to its more modern incarnations, the Royal Green Jackets and now The Rifles. Charting the numerous conflicts and battles in which they were involved. Thorough descriptions are accompanied by colour pictures, regimental timelines and maps.
