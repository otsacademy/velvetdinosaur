Watch this talk with David Innes and Richard Folkes streamed live from the Soldiers of Oxfordshire Museum, in association with The Army Flying Museum.

Featuring personal, family recollections and archival recordings of these inspiring men, hear the compelling stories of Major John Howard and SSgt Geoff Barkway from that historic morning at the bridges over the Caen canal and Orne river. This is the Pegasus Bridge story in their own words.

Live stream is free to view, no booking or registration required. Donations welcome during the stream in support of the museum charity.
