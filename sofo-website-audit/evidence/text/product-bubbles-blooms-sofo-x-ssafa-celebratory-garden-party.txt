Soldiers of Oxfordshire Trust and SSAFA: The Armed Forces Charity present our celebratory summer Garden Party.

Drinks and canapés served throughout, listen to jazz from the Waterloo Band of The Rifles and enjoy exclusive ‘behind the scenes’ tours of the SOFO archives, the From Battlefields to Blooms flower show and the new exhibition, Lee Miller: Woman at War.

---

Additional event parking will be available at Blenheim Palace for this event.

Sat Nav: OX20 1UL
What3Words: ///village.widely.handover

Blenheim Palace parking is approximately 8 minutes walk from the Museum Gardens.

Proceeds from the sale of your ticket(s) directly support SSAFA, the armed forces charity, and Soldiers of Oxfordshire Trust.
