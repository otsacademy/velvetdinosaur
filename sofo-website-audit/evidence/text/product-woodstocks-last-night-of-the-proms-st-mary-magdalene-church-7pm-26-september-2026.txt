Featuring the Waterloo Band & Bugles of The Rifles, this 'Last Night of the Proms'-style live concert kicks off from 7pm at Woodstock's St Mary Magdalene Church.

Proceeds from ticket sales support the St Mary Magdalene Church Roof Fund and Soldiers of Oxfordshire Trust.

---

The Waterloo Band and Bugles of The Rifles is part of 7 RIFLES and rehearse in theirown purpose-built
modern band block on the same site as A Coy at Edward Brooks Baracks, Abingdon.

The Waterloo Band & Bugles is a flexible and versatile ensemble and have provided musical support for
a wide range of occassions, from their 35 piece concert band to marching bands and quintets for smaller venues both locally and around the world.
