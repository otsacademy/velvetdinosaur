Alongside the exhibition of his work at the museum, Remembrances of War: Between Memory, Imagination and Reality Paul Joyce is kindly donating one of his original pieces, Worm in the Bud, to be raffled in support of the museum charity, Soldiers of Oxfordshire Trust.

A painter, photographer and filmmaker, Paul Joyce’s artwork is held in a number of prominent private and public collections, including The V&A, The National Portrait Gallery, The Tate Gallery, and the National Museum of Wales.

Enter online here, or purchase a ticket at the museum Gift Shop during your visit. A winner will be drawn at random from all entries. The contact details you provide when purchasing your ticket(s) online will be used to contact you in the event that you are the lucky winner.

After purchasing online, you will receive confirmation emails and a link to download a digital raffle ticket with the draw details.
