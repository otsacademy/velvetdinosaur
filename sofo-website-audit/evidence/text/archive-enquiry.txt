SUBMIT YOUR RESEARCH ENQUIRY

Use the online form below to submit your enquiry, or download a copy of the form and email or post it to us using the details below.

Please make sure you have read the details on our main Research Page before submitting your enquiry, or contact us for more information if unsure.

If there is more information potentially available and therefore more time is needed, you will be advised in advance. In some cases this may result in a quotation for additional work.

To start your research enquiry please choose which type of search you would like, then complete at least 2 parts in section 1.

You can view an example of a completed Enquiry Report here.

Download and fill out a copy of our Research Service Form: (Download PDF) (Download Word Document)

Email a copy of your completed form to us at frontofhouse@sofo.org.uk. You can still pay for Research Enquiry fees online using the listing on our website store.

Alternatively, completed forms can be posted to us at: Research Service, SOFO Office, Harrisons Lane, Woodstock, OX20 1SN along with a cheque for the enquiry fee made out to 'Soldiers of Oxfordshire Museum'.
