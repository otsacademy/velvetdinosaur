A fragment of a First World War Zeppelin and engine starting handle. The airship was brought down over Essex on 24th September 1916.
The items come from either of Zeppelins L32 or L33. Both were part of the Zeppelin group (L31, L32, L33 and L34) that bombed London and surrounding counties on the night of 23 September 1916.
