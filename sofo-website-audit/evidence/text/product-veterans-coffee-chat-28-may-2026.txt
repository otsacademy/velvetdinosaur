Soldiers of Oxfordshire Museum's veterans' coffee mornings run on the fourth Thursday of every month from 10am - 12pm.

Enjoy complimentary tea, coffee and biscuits alongside the chance to catch up with old friends and meet a few new ones. Open to all veterans, free of charge.

The museum galleries will also be open for veterans to browse free of charge during these events.

Kindly supported by The Veterans' Foundation.
