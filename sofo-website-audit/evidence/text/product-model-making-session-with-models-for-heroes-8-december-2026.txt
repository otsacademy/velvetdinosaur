Drop in for Model Making at the Museum! Theses regular sessions are open to all and run from 11am - 2pm every second Tuesday of the month.

Feel free to bring along your own project to work on, or if you have never tried modelling before, find out more about the hobby and have a go. No need to book, just drop in.

Models for Heroes support UK Armed Forces and Emergency Service personnel with the meaningful hobby of model making.
