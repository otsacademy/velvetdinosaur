OBJECT OF THE MONTH – DECEMBER 2018

Try to guess what the theme is for December's OOTM... of course it's Christmas!

In previous OOTMs I have tried to stick with a theme for each month, which has proved fairly difficult for some months. Luckily, we have many Christmas cards sent from and to Oxfordshire soldiers in our archive, so I was rather spoiled for choice.

I also featured a Princess Mary tin, with 1914, 1918 and 2018 Christmas cards, which I thought offered a nice comparison.

In 2019 I will be introducing a 'My Favourite Object' case which will be displayed for a longer period than Object of the Month and will feature the favourite objects of SOFO staff and volunteers. Look out for these posts in 2019!
