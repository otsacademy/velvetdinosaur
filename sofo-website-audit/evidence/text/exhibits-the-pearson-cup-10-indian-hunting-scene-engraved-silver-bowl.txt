The Pearson Cup: Indian 19th Century Hunting Scene Engraved Silver Bowl

'ELLO

19th Century Indian silver bowl, decorated with dancers, court and hunting scenes. Cups like this often awarded to officers during inter-regimental competitions in areas such as hunting and shooting. The cup measures 10 inches at the rim and weighs 76 ounces.
