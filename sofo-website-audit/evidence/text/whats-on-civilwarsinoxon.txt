The Civil Wars in Oxfordshire: Human Costs and Consequences
Online Talk and Live Q&A with Prof. Andrew Hopper
Live from 7pm GMT Thursday 25 January 2024

 

THE CIVIL WARS IN OXFORDSHIRE: HUMAN COSTS AND CONSEQUENCES

In this online talk and live Q&A, Professor Andrew Hopper  will explore the people's experience of the Civil Wars in the city of Oxford and the county of Oxfordshire. It will uncover many local human-interest stories of loss, suffering and survival. It will pay particular attention to those wounded, widowed and orphaned by the war, drawn from the recently completed Civil War Petitions Project.

No booking or pre-registration required to view this talk, the video feed will be available on this page once the talk goes live, and will remain available on-demand shortly after the live event ends.
