MAKE A PAPER MOSAIC OF POPPIES - COMPETITION WINNERS!

We're delighted to announce that the winners of our competition are:

5th Didcot Brownies, Squirrel Six, for the older age group, with their wonderful depiction of the iconic WW1 soldier standing with poppies

and

5th Abingdon Rainbows, Group Three, for the younger age group. They made a very clever design interweaving poppies round a cross.

We had over 100 entries and the standard was amazing!

Each of the children involved in the winning entries receives a family annual pass to the Museum.
