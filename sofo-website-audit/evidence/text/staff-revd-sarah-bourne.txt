 Revd Sarah Bourne
 Trustee

Sarah Bourne is the Rector of Woodstock and Bladon, having previously served as Chaplain for the Arts at Banbury St Mary, at Coventry Cathedral, and in the Benefice of Shipston-on Stour where she was also RBL Chaplain for 8 years.

After reading Classics at Cambridge University, Sarah started her working life as a school teacher of Classics (Latin, Greek and Classical Civilisation), and as a keen performing musician, she has also taught oboe and piano. She has a particular interest in using the arts to engage with local communities.
