KIDS CLUB

Once a month on Wednesdays after school and on Friday mornings in the holidays, activity sessions for girls and boys aged 6-9 years.

No Parents! Packed with trails, quizzes, games, craft, active learning, kitchen science and outdoor fun.

A new theme each session including topics inspired by the Museum, the seasons and the best of the school curriculum.

Booking essential! See What's On for details and click through to book. For more information, contact the Education Officer on 01993 810211 or email education@sofo.org.uk
