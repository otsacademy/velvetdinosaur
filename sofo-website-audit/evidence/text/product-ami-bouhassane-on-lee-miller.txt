A talk with Ami Bouhassane, author and co-director of Farleys House & Gallery, on the work and legacy of her grandmother, Lee Miller - celebrating the Lee Miller: A Woman at War exhibition at the Soldiers of Oxfordshire Museum (23 May - 13 September 2026).

Farleys House & Gallery Ltd is the organisation managing the Lee Miller Archives, the Penrose Collection and Farleys House - the home of her grandparents, Lee Miller and Roland Penrose.

About Ami Bouhassane

Managing Director of Farleys House & Gallery Ltd, the organisation that manages the Lee Miller Archives and Farleys House, home of her grandparents Lee Miller and Roland Penrose. Through Farleys Bouhassane organises exhibitions in connection with their work, collections and contemporaries that are exhibited internationally as well as in the gallery spaces on site. Author, curator, podcast presenter and lecturer on both Miller and Penrose, Bouhassane has worked extensively with their material for over 28 years.

Her first book ‘Lee Miller: A Life with Food, Friends and Recipes (2017) won 3 awards, including the IACP (International Association of Culinary Professionals) Jane Grigson award for scholarship. She is currently recording her third podcast series based on manuscripts and letters written by Miller held in the Lee Miller Archives.
