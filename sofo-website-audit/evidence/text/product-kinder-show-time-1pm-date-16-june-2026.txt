Original listing:

Show Time: 1pm 16 June 2026 (This show is intended for school and community group bookings)

Escaping on a Kindertransport train, one small Czech-Jewish girl embarks on a mighty adventure.

Crossing between the past and present, Babi travels across Europe; from bon bons in Germany to the smell of the sea in Margate, she discovers how tiny acts of kindness can change the course of a person’s life.

Joyful, moving and poignant, Kinder features beautiful table-top puppetry and cinematic shadow play, as Babi tries to assemble the parts of her broken identity, to find peace in her future.

Kinder is a multi-award winning show, from puppetry and visual theatre company Smoking Apples. Inviting you to take a seat inside an immersive set, the story unfolds through a series of playful hatches and openings.

“Unmissable” – ✮✮✮✮✮ Everything Theatre

A Smoking Apples production, aimed at ages 9 - 18 years.

Supported using public funding by the National Lottery through Arts Council England and Enterprise Arts Trust.

Age Suitability

Kinder is best suited to ages 9 +. The show is ideal for ages 9 - 18, with ages 9 - 11 (Years 5 & 6) learning about the Holocaust, as part of the curriculum and ages 12 - 18 (Years 7 - 13) studying Drama, English, History and/or Religious Studies.

The show contains some complex references to war and persecution, which younger audiences may find challenging. Kinder runs for up to an hour, without an interval and for the duration, the audience are seated

Accessibility

Kinder has been designed to be accessible for neurodivergent audiences. In the space, we will be providing stims (stimulatory accessories) and active responses to the show are encouraged. Audiences will be able to leave and re-enter the show, at any time and there will be a chill out area (with bean bags, ear defenders and more stims) for periods of regulation. An inclusive resource pack is available on the Smoking Apples thetre website in advance and includes a social story. Soldiers of Oxfordshire Museum also has its own social story available on our Visit Us page.

The show and venue is wheelchair accessible, providing that we are informed in advance so that we can take into account the seating arrangement and adjust audience numbers accordingly.
