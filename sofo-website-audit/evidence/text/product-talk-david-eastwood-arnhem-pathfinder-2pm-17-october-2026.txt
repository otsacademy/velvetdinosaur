Join James Wilkins, Community Curator at Soldiers of Oxfordshire Museum, for a companion talk to the new exhibition, which tells the compelling story of Lt. David Eastwood MC.

Commissioned into the Oxfordshire & Buckinghamshire Light Infantry, Eastwood served as Pathfinder for 1st Parachute Brigade during Operation Market Garden - his corage and service forming part of the dramtic story of the Battle of Arnhem.

In this talk, James will explore his role in the operation, the wider airborne campaign and his strong Oxfordshire links.

Our Saturday talks include day admission to the museum on the date of the talk, leave time on the dat to explore our galleries. Galleries open 11am - 5pm.
