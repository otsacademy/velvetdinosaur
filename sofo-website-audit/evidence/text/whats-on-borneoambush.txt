ONLINE TALK: ‘JUNIOR PARTICIPANTS IN A SMALL WAR’

Our latest online talk takes you back to 1964, in the midst of the Indonesian Confrontation, as we hear of the experiences of a young 1st Green Jackets (43rd & 52nd) rifle platoon commander on active service as he prepares for and carries out an ambush operation in the jungle of the Borneo-Indonesia border.
