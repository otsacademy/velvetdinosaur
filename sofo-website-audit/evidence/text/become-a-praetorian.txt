BECOME A PRÆTORIAN

Our Prætorians are vital to the success of the museum and helped raise the funds, through membership and events, which enabled the museum to open its galleries in 2014.
Receiving all the benefits of our Friends membership, Prætorians are also welcomed to an annual lunch event with opportunities to meet the Board, Staff and Volunteers, often in interesting county locations and with speakers who reflect the diverse military heritage of the county.

The benefits of becoming a Prætorian

Free admission to the museum
10% discount in the museum shop
Invitations to our new exhibition openings
Exclusive annual AGM and social event at an interesting county location
Membership card
Quarterly newsletter
Invitations to our lectures and events

How much is membership?

£30 per month
£90 per quater
£360 per year
