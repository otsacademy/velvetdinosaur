Drop in 11am - 2pm during the 82nd anniversary of D-Day and explore history up close! This special object handling session lets you go hands-on with objects and equipment that would have been used by soldiers serving in Normandy in 1944. Discover their stories of bravery - including the vital capture of Pegasus Bridge.

Open to all ages.

Drop-in included with museum admission. Standard admissions include 12 months entry to the museum galleries. See www.sofo.org.uk/visit-us for all admission prices and offers. Galleries open 11am - 5pm on Saturday 6 June 2026.
