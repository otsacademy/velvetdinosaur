Help Us Grow The Oxfordshire Military Family Tree
We’re looking at our future and we want YOU to be part of it.

Oxfordshire's Military Family Tree being installed in the museum's gallery

Whilst we were closed to visitors during the UK Lockdown in 2020-2021, we worked on projects to enhance the museum displays and create new opportunities to mark the contributions of those individuals who have done so much for the county in times of conflict and peace.

We are creating the county's Military Family Tree inside the museum and each person will be represented and remembered by a tile that tells a little of their story. You might have a family connection with one of the county regiments or simply want to commemorate an individual whose story is important to you.

By donating a minimum of £150, your personalised tile will be created and displayed.

Each donation helps us look after the archive and keep this unique historical resource safe. Your £150 will allow us to buy conservation materials or create resources for family learning and school workshops.

Donations can be made online through the Charities Aid Foundation (CAF).
