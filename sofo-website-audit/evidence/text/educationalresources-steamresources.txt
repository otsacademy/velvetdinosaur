STEAM LEARNING ACTIVITIES & RESOURCES

These resources, aimed at Key Stages 1 to 3 have been made available with support from The Army Museums Ogilby Trust. Discover a range of activities with curriculum links to use in the classroom, at home and settings such as Brownies and Scouts. All of them come from our museum resources and we hope you enjoy finding out more about STEAM in our archive. If you would like us to support your STEAM learning please email Kim Davies engagement@sofo.org.uk
