SOFO Archive Galleries

Below is a library of our archive galleries. Each gallery contains images that you can purchase as high-resolution downloads for either personal or commercial use.

Once you have completed your purchase, you will be emailed with login details to download your images.

Soldiers of Oxfordshire Museum Education Collection: WW2 Evacuees
Images of objects from the museum's education collection, used in handling sessions and educational resources
Wyld Atlas - Peninsular War Maps
James Wyld's Atlas with detailed maps of Peninsular War battles. c.1840
Winston Churchill - Queens Own Oxfordshire Hussar
Images related to Winston Churchill' service with the Queens Own Oxfordshire Hussars (Oxfordshire Yeomanry). Pre-1914.
