About

Updated May 27, 2026

 Penny Stocks
 Trustee

Penny Stocks started working in the Car Industry in the 1980s and then spent 20 years working as a management consultant becoming Partner in the Global Accounting firm EY.

She specialised in human resources and change management helping organisations transform and improve revenues and productivity. Her experience is cross sector ranging from the public sector, to pharmaceutical and infrastructure.

Penny moved to Woodstock 5 years ago and joined the museum as a volunteer in 2025, before becoming a Trustee in early 2026. Although never having served in the military many of Penny’s family and friends have served which has resulted in a deep respect for those who have.
