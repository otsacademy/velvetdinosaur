Remembered: The Men on the War Memorials of Witney, Crawley and Hailey. Volume 2: 1939-1945. quantity
Add to Cart

Jeff Clements gives an extensive and full description of the stories of individual mean named on the Witney, Crawley and Hailey World War 2 memorials. Featuring multiple diary entry accounts, as well as a number of photographs of the soldiers alongside a contextual description of what was happening on various fronts at the time they served.

Paperback, 170 pages.
