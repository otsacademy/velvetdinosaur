BYDUNCAN PARSONS// ABOUT US, VISIT US
Join us at one of the regular Veterans’ coffee mornings at Soldiers of Oxfordshire Museum
Veterans’ Coffee & Chat
We’re starting a new, regular veterans’ coffee morning at the museum. Starting on Thursday 28 September the event will become a bi-monthly fixture, with future dates falling on the final Thursday of every other month.

You’ll find us across the garden from The Oxfordshire Museum and Woodstock Library

Open to all veterans, they’ll offer an opportunity to meet up and catch up with fellow veterans as well as a chance to browse the museum galleries free of charge.
With the support of Models for Heroes, there will also be time to unwind with a spot of military model making.

A range of models you’ll see on display at the museum – courtesy of a volunteer

2024 Dates: 25th January , 28 March, 30 May, 25 July , 26 September, 28 November

 Tags: Armed Forces, coffee, coffee morning, drop in, model making, models, Oxfordshire, UK Armed Forces, veteran, Veterans, Woodstock
