Our Armed Forces Day event at the Soldiers of Oxfordshire Museum is designed to recognise and celebrate the vital contributions of the UK’s Armed Forces. The Armed Forces community, from current personnel and veterans to service families and cadets, will have free access to the exhibitions. There will be stalls for cadets to promote their activities, living history displays and activities for families.

Entry is free for veterans, armed forces personnel and their families. Donations welcome on the day!
