Ways to Support Us

Now more than ever it is crucial that we raise additional funds in order to survive. While we largely rely on visitors to the museum, there are many other way you can support Soldiers of Oxfordshire Trust in our work preserving the county's military heritage, including the many personal stories and objects in our museum and archives.

Shop with SOFO

Any purchase you make in our Gift Shop or via our website store supports the museum, but you can also support the museum when shopping elsewhere - at no cost to you!

 
Did you know you can help Soldiers of Oxfordshire Trust while you do your online shopping? Simply go to www.easyfundraising.co.uk and create an account. Choose SOFO Trust as your charity and whenever you shop with one of their partner retailers, SOFO receives a donation. It really is as easy as that. The best bit is it doesn't cost you a penny!
