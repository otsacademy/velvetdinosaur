Our County At War - The Home Front in Buckinghamshire 1914 - 18 DVD quantity
Add to Cart

Depiction of life in Buckinghamshire during the Great War. Through an extraordinary range of original visual material from all round the county, it shows the how the war has impacted everyone’s lives.

Bucks lost over 8,000 men. Back home, the images that are conveyed show the disruption to family life, how the role of women changed, and how children played their part in the war effort.

Produced and Directed by Joanna Barclay
