Meet Me at the Soldiers Museum is our regular drop-in for older adults, join us and enjoy a cup of tea, a chat, and reminisce as you browse the museum galleries.

We particularly welcome groups from care and retirement homes. If you would like to attend one of these sessions with a group of residents, please get in touch.

Free entry, refreshments provided.

Running 2pm - 4pm, every Thursday during school term times (No sessions during the Easter and Summer holidays).

These events have been kindly supported by University of Oxford Gardens, Libraries & Museums.
