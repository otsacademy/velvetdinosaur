Primary Schools

Our learning sessions use artefacts, documents, film and sound and focus on either the First World War or the Home Front during the Second World War.
Sessions can be delivered at the museum and or at school as part of our outreach service.

Sessions available

First World War – ‘Your Country Needs You!’

Second World War – ‘Don’t You Know There’s a War On!’
