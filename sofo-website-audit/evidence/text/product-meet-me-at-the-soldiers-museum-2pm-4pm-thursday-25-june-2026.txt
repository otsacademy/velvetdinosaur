Meet Me at the Soldiers Museum is our regular reminiscence drop-in for older adults.

This special edition of Meet Me at the Soldiers Museum features a film screening of the award-winning 1942 classic, Mrs. Miniver.

Drop in, see the film, and walk back into the '40s with our Lee Miller photography exhibition.

Free entry, refreshments provided.

Screening begins at 2pm.

Regular sessions run from 2pm - 4pm, every Thursday during school term times (No sessions during the Easter and Summer holidays).

These events have been kindly supported by University of Oxford Gardens, Libraries & Museums.
