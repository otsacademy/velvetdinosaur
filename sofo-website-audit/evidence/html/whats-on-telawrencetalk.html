SOFO Online Talks – T. E. Lawrence

T.E. Lawrence - The Unconventional Warrior

Online Talk and Live Q&A with Brigadier Hugh Willing CBE

Now Available

Online Talk: T.E. Lawrence The Unconventional Warrior

Join the Live Q&A

To the war-weary British in 1916, TE Lawrence rapidly became a hero - “Lawrence of Arabia” - the man who seemed a gallant brother to the Arabic people fighting to gain freedom from their Turkish overlords. But were Lawrence’s motives as clear-cut as the legend suggests? This talk will explore who T.E. Lawrence really was.
